# Code of Conduct

Status: stub. A full code of conduct will be adopted before the contribution interface opens (Phase 5 in `PROJECT_BRIEF.md`).

## Standards

Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- Engage respectfully and professionally
- Focus on technical substance and verifiable claims
- Disclose affiliations and conflicts of interest where relevant (especially for vendor mappings)
- Cite primary sources and avoid misrepresenting third-party material
- Accept and offer constructive criticism in good faith

The following are not acceptable:

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language
- Misrepresenting affiliation or authorship
- Submitting vendor mappings without disclosing affiliation
- Knowingly submitting inaccurate citations or unverifiable claims

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces, including the GitHub repository, issues, pull requests, and any associated communication channels.

## Enforcement

Concerns may be raised by opening an issue or contacting the maintainer directly. The maintainer will respond and act in good faith. Serious or repeated violations may result in removal of contributions and exclusion from the project.

This stub will be replaced with a full code of conduct (likely based on the Contributor Covenant or a comparable standard) before the contribution interface opens.
